This interactive map shows all 63 US National Parks and over 100 National Monuments. Parks marked with green pins are ones I've been fortunate enough to visit, while monuments are shown with blue pins. Use the layer control in the top-right to toggle the display of National Monuments. Click on any marker to see details about that location.
